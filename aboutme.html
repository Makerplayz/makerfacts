About Me

Hey, I'm Ephream (Makerplays)

I'm a content creator, Minecraft builder, gamer, and digital developer. My journey started with a passion for building—from playing with Legos and designing courses in Super Mario Maker to learning how to code computer software.

That drive to construct and create inspired the handle Makerplays. Over the past three years, I've expanded into managing a multi-channel network covering Minecraft survival series, build battles, VR experiences, and short-form gaming memes across YouTube and TikTok.

MakerFacts serves as the central directory, landing page, and legal hub connecting all of my channels, servers, and ongoing creator projects in one clean place.

What I Do

Creating series like the Epic Build Battle and Breaking Minecraft PE Edition, while running custom SMP and community server spaces.

🎨 Content Creation
