About Me

Hey, I'm Ephream (Makerplays)

I’ve wanted to be a YouTuber for the past eight years, ever since I was six years old. I didn’t actually start making content until I was 11, when I uploaded a video called "Roblox Roller Coaster and Get Eaten." It was on my parents' YouTube account, and unfortunately, it has since been deleted. I uploaded five more videos after that before quitting for a while.

When I turned 12, I decided to get back into it. This time, I wanted a channel name that was interesting—something that would make everyone want to subscribe to me. That's how I came up with "Makerplays." I chose "Maker" because I loved Super Mario Maker, Legos, and even coding, for crying out loud! The "plays" part came from watching a bunch of YouTubers like UnspeakablePlays and PrestonPlayz.

Once I stuck with that name, I decided to work on a channel logo. At first, my profile picture was just an image of xNestorio, which I used until I reached 35 subscribers. After that milestone, I finally created my first real logo: a black panther with the text "Maker" on top and "plays" on the bottom.

I started out making block puzzle videos until they stopped getting views, so I decided to quit doing them altogether. Instead, I started a new Minecraft journey called 'Breaking Minecraft PE Edition'. That first video got 200 views, which made me think, 'Okay, I'm definitely making more of these!'

That's when I decided to do my first collab ever with EpicGamerQMC. When we first met, we asked each other, 'Do you have a YouTube channel?' We both said yes, and right then and there, we decided to collab entirely!

I stuck with Minecraft for a while before thinking, 'Maybe I could do Roblox content too?' So, I started making both Roblox and Minecraft videos. Later down the line, I branched out into Call of Duty, Ranking videos, Shorts, and more.

But the one series that truly started it all was the 'Makerplays Epic Build Battle Series'—which is famous for pitting YouTubers against viewers. Episode 1, which featured LukeHDPlays vs. CannedNoodles, pulled in 400 views! Because of that success, by Episode 2, I started using my new signature intro: 'Heya, I'm Makerplays, also known as the guy who does the Makerplays Epic Build Battle Series!' Now, it doesn't even matter if I'm actually filming a Build Battle or not... that's just my official introduction.

Later on, I started creating separate channels for different types of content: Roblox (MakerGamez), VR (MakerVR), Memes (MakerMEMES), and of course, Minecraft and Call of Duty stayed on the main channel (Makerplays). It used to host all of my content, but after a post I made here, things changed entirely.
